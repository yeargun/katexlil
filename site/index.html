Two contracts

            
Open world,
closed world.

          

          

            Open world is the reusable library: the public ESM names, the option-object keys and every
            JavaScript-visible field stay as written, so renderToString(tex, {displayMode: true}) works
            unchanged. Its fair opponent is Terser, Oxc or esbuild with identifier mangling on and property mangling off.
            Closed world is the same source under extern_fields = false: fields the compiler
            owns may be renamed, the way Closure ADVANCED renames an app. Configs:
            lilscript.toml,
            lilscript.closed.toml.

Source maps, both lanes

            
Where the
bytes go.

          

          

            The compiler emits a Source Map v3 for its artifact, and the official lane is rebuilt from the Flow sources with
            esbuild and Terser maps composed, so every token on both sides names its module. Per module: the raw bytes it owns,
            and its marginal Brotli-11 cost (the artifact minus that module's tokens). Positive is where this port is bigger.
            Tool: scripts/attribute-map.mjs.
